Boxing Sim

Avyukth Selvadurai



Description

This game is meant to be a simple but immersive experience created to teach the basics of boxing through interactive gameplay and mechanics.


Motivation

To make use of all the things we learned in class: physically based audio, virtual object interaction, IRL -> VR locomotion.
Game design is my focus not research so being able to learn new things in Unity is very important to me.



Proposed Work:

  • Short Term Goals

    • Smooth tracking of hands with controller.
    • Smooth tracking of hands without controller.
    • Simple audio interaction (audio clip plays) on punch impact.
    • Immersive environment and scene setup with light, textures, and materials.

  • Medium Term goals

    • Full body tracking.
    • IRL Locomotion -> VR Movement (walking around IRL moves you in game).
    • Physically based audio.
    • Activity where you can combine these features to learn basic boxing skills.

  • Long Term goals

    • Full body tracking -> translation of movement to a 3D model ingame.
    • Teaching basic moves through simulation.

Completed Work:

  • Short Term Goals

    • Hands are tracked with and without the controller. Velocity is only displayed while using the controller.
    • An audio clip of a punch plays on impact with the boxing bag. Its sound level scales with punch impact.
    • Two environments have been created, one boxing ring, one simple training setting which multiple scenes use.

  • Medium Term goals

    • IRL Locomotion has been implemented. The scene has simply been set to a stage which enables movement.
    • Audio is physically based in the scenes using steamaudio and steamaudio materials.
    • Multiple training scenes have been created:
      • Main scene that teaches you your stance (orthodox/southpaw)
      • Punching dummy scene that teaches you two punches (jab (1) and cross (2)) along with a simple 1, 2 combination
      • Realistics punching bag scene that shows basic statistics like velocity and hit position

  • Long Term goals

    • The groundwork for teaching basic moves through the game has been implemented. While its efficacy isn't as high as I would like it to be it exists.
